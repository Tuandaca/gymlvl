# Bundled fonts

Downloaded from the Google Fonts source repository. Original licenses are included next to the font files.

- [InterVariable.ttf](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/inter/Inter%5Bopsz%2Cwght%5D.ttf)
- [InterItalicVariable.ttf](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/inter/Inter-Italic%5Bopsz%2Cwght%5D.ttf)
- [Inter-OFL.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/inter/OFL.txt)
- [OrbitronVariable.ttf](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/orbitron/Orbitron%5Bwght%5D.ttf)
- [Orbitron-OFL.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/orbitron/OFL.txt)

The bundled `ArchivoVariable.ttf`, `SpaceGroteskVariable.ttf`,
`SpaceMono-Regular.ttf`, and `SpaceMono-Bold.ttf` are currently relabeled
Inter-derived files, not the typefaces named by their filenames. Their glyph
outlines and horizontal metrics match `InterVariable.ttf` byte for byte; the
two `SpaceMono` files are proportional. Inter provenance is documented above.
Keep these files unchanged until a
separate typography and licensing decision approves a replacement or a
truthful family mapping.
